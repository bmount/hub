    Pimwell at a glance.

    Pimwell is a shared workplace for a small team and the AI helpers that work alongside it. You can always see what happened and why, and change what happens next to fit the people you serve.

    Knows what your code did

    Every change, followed into production: what it broke, what it fixed, and what came after.

    Knows how long it takes

    How long each change takes to run, so a slowdown points straight at the change behind it.

    Knows who noticed

    The users a change reached, and what they wrote to you about it.

    Knows why it exists

    Each change links to the request behind it, and to the person or AI helper who made it.

    Knows what it earns

    What the people using each feature spend, and what they generate for you.

    Changes from your own Claude or ChatGPT

    Ask for a fix in the assistant you already use. A helper makes it, and you can see every step.

    A room for every team

    Each team gets its own address, people and projects. Nothing finished is thrown away.

    Small, cheap, and yours

    It costs little to run, and any project packs into one file you can keep.

    Pimwell is private, for now.

    It's invite-only while we build it. If someone sent you an invite link, open it to move in.
